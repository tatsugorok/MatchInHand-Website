90-second overview

The whole match in four moves

  1. 01

    Shape your team

    Place five Player cards across Defense, Midfield and Attack. Your formation limits which Action cards you can play.

  2. 02

    Draw

    Start each turn by taking the top card from the Draw pile.

  3. 03

    Play or pass

    Play one or more cards, or play nothing and draw a second card.

  4. 04

    Score more

    Launch attacks, match them with defense and finish regular time with more goals than your opponent.

Before kick-off

Set the table

One central Draw pile, one Goal pile and a five-player formation for each side.

  1. 1

    Deal the action cards

    Shuffle all 60 orange, green and blue Action cards. Deal 5 face down to each player, then place the rest face down as the Draw pile.

  2. 2

    Mark half-time

    Insert the Half-Time card in the middle of the Draw pile.

  3. 3

    Give each player a bench

    Give each player 2 Substitutions, 1 Formation Switch, 1 VAR Goal Check and 1 Out of Position card. Keep these Management cards beside the hand.

  4. 4

    Build both teams

    Give 5 Home Player cards to one player and 5 Away Player cards to the other. Turn the remaining Player cards over to form the Goal pile.

  5. 5

    Kick off

    The Home Team starts. You can also decide the first player with a coin toss.

Your tactical shape

Formation controls your options

Each Player card occupies Defense, Midfield or Attack. The number in a line is the maximum number of matching Action cards you can play at once.

Your starting formation must place at least one Player in every position. Once the match begins, Management cards can break that restriction.

The match engine

The attack-defense phase

Only the active player can start an attack. Once it begins, both players are part of the same turn until the duel is resolved.

Attacker

Set the attack value

Start by playing one or more +Attack cards together. Once the attack is initiated, you cannot add more +Attack cards or trigger a second attack.

VS
Defender

Match the attack

Play enough +Defense to match the Attack value, use an effect that reduces it, or deliberately concede without spending cards.

01Goal

The defense falls short. The scorer records a goal.

02Stalemate

The defense succeeds and neither side plays another card.

03Counter

A successful defense becomes an attack. The exchange can continue back and forth.

GOAL!

The player who concedes starts the next turn and draws two cards instead of one.

Regular time

Two halves. One winner.

HT
01

First half

Play until the Half-Time card is revealed on top of the Draw pile. Stop drawing, complete the current turn, then play one final turn.

HT

Half-time break

Rebuild your entire formation for free with no restrictions. If you hold fewer than 5 Action cards, draw back up to 5.

02

Second half

The player who did not start the first half kicks off. When the Draw pile is depleted, finish that turn and play one final turn.

Most goals after regular time wins.

If the score is level

Extra time

  1. Reshuffle the Action cards and set up as usual.
  2. Split the Draw pile in half. Put the Half-Time card into one half and discard the other.
  3. Keep any unused Management cards; do not redistribute them.
  4. The player who started regular time starts extra time.

Settle it from the spot

Penalty shoot-out

  1. Give each player 2 Goal Chance cards and 1 Miraculous Save. Line them up face down.
  2. The opponent chooses one card to reveal. A Save stops the penalty; either Goal Chance scores.
  3. Take 5 penalties each. If level, continue in sudden death until one player wins.

Referee’s decision

FAQ & timing

When can Instant cards be played?

Whenever an opponent plays a card, unless the card says otherwise. Penalty! is played immediately after a successful defense or Tactical Foul and before any additional card. Tactical Foul can stop a Counter-Attack if it is played just before the Counter and the opponent defended with +Defense cards.

Can I add Attack cards after an attack begins?

No. Choose the complete initial Attack value when you launch the attack. Once initiated, you cannot add more +Attack cards or trigger a second attack.

How do Counter-Attacks interact with other cards?

A Counter-Attack can be boosted by Overlap, but not by adding +Attack cards. Once the Counter-Attack is played, the Defense cards used before it can no longer be negated. An Instant card can still stop the counter if played immediately before it.

Do Overlap and Out of Position work retroactively?

No. Their effects only apply to cards played together with them, never to cards already used earlier in the turn.

What exactly can Tactical Foul negate?

If it negates an Attack card, the opponent cannot add more Attack cards. If it negates a Defense card, the opponent may continue defending. It cannot negate a card effect that has already resolved during the turn.

What happens when regular time ends level?

Players can agree to a draw, play Extra Time and then a shoot-out if needed, or go directly to the Penalty Shoot-Out.
